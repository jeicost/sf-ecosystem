Slide flow: 1) Welcome / Glove up 2) Pick your salsa / Match the mood 3) Make it yours / Pour with confidence 4) Take the first bite 5) Pass it to your crew 6) Join the ritual

FORMAT NOTE WHY ALBUMS WIN FOR RITUAL Albums stop the scroll with the first image. Each slide = one ritual step. The swipe = the actual experience. Saves rate on ritual content is 3-4× higher than single posts. This is our most memorable format.

SALSA PHRASES
Conversion · Brand Cult
Shareable, funny, locally-relevant phrases that people tag their crew in. No product required — just the brand voice. Designed to spread via Story shares. Local humour, Thai context, universal appetite.
Formats: Single pic · FB/IG/TikTok · No CTA needed — share IS the action
APRIL — Content 10 SALSA PHRASES SERIES Active phrases in rotation:
· "DIP IT LIKE YOU MEAN IT"
· "DIP NOW THINK LATER"
· "YOUR HANGOVER FAVOURITE IN THAILAND"

Concept: replicate accounts that post funny shareable phrases — always food or music-related, with local Bangkok humour. Also run in Thai.
FORMAT RULE BOLD TYPE, DARK BG, ONE LINE Maximum visual impact = minimum words. Anton or Impact font. Full bleed dark background. Orange accent. The phrase IS the post. No food photo needed. Highest share rate of any format.

Formats: Photo album · FB/IG · Monthly cadence · Educate + entertain

Did you know he studied at Berklee College of Music? Won +20 Latin Grammy Awards. From the Caribbean to the world — no shortcuts, just rhythm and soul. A TRUE SALSA ICON."

Hits featured: Burbujas de Amor · Ojalá que Llueva Café · La Bilirrubina
SERIES CONCEPT MONTHLY ROTATION Each icon connects to the brand's DNA: Latino energy + bold personality + cultural crossover. Not always musicians — could be chefs, athletes, filmmakers. The link is always spirit, not genre. Fans start guessing who's next.

SALSA ICONIC MOMENTS
Awareness · Cultural

Formats: Photo album (6 slides) · FB/IG · "Which moment should we remix next?" CTA

Caption: "Some moments changed the game. Ours just made it saucier. Welcome to Sauce Moments — where icons get the Salsa Burgers treatment."
CREATIVE RULE INSPIRED-BY, NOT EXACT Use recognisable body pose / action / visual reference, but replace ball/trophy with burger or sauce. Focus on the energy of the moment, not the person. Brand-safe. Culturally alive. Legally clean. Fan engagement: "Which moment should we remix next?"
