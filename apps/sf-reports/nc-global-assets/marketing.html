● URGENT

● HIGH

● MEDIUM

Vite+React renders on the client side. While Google has improved JS crawling, a pre-rendered or SSR version guarantees full indexability. Combined with 5+ quality blog articles targeting "Thailand market entry" keywords, this becomes a consistent inbound lead channel within 3 months.
