## 2. Sauce Science

```
Heat is not a flavor. It's a distraction.

Most sauces hide behind chili and call it a personality. Ours don't need to.

A sauce has four jobs on Wagyu: cut the fat, carry the salt, wake up the sweet, hold the finish. Miss one and the whole bite falls flat. Nail all four and you understand why we make 18 of them from scratch every morning — no premixes, ever.

So before you reach for the hottest one, ask what your bite actually needs. Fat to cut? Go acid. Beef too quiet? Go umami. Craving loud? That's what heat is for — the exclamation mark, never the sentence.
```
