# Brand brief — example input for the strategist

**Brand:** Salsa Burgers
**What they sell:** Premium sauce-first Wagyu burgers, delivery-first. 18 house sauces made fresh daily.
**Signature experience:** "The Salsa Ritual" — black gloves, open the box, pour your finishing sauce, first bite, share.
**Market / audience:** Sathorn, Bangkok. 70% expats + internationals (25–42, Instagram-first, Grab-heavy),
30% urban Thais (22–35, TikTok-first, trend-driven). They want a burger that's an event, not just food.
**Differentiator:** Sauce-first + the ritual. No other brand in Bangkok owns the sauce experience.
**Tone / voice:** Bold, irreverent, imperative. Every sentence is a command ("DIP NOW", "GLOVE UP").
Never "delicious" or "best". Bilingual EN/ES flavor.
**Tagline:** DIP IT LIKE YOU MEAN IT
**Handle:** @salsaburgers.bkk
**Channels:** Instagram, TikTok, Facebook. Order via Grab & LINE MAN.
**Promo:** 20% off first order, code SALSA1.
**Menu highlights:** Mala Burger (Szechuan mala glaze) 520, Truffle Flow 500, Khao Soi/Tom Yum/Holy Basil
local-flavor burgers 500, OG Cheeseburger 420, Crunchy Chicken 460, Lobster Burger 2800, Salsa Duo combo,
Signature Shakes, Basque Cheesecake.
**Colors:** near-black + bright brand red + cream.
**Do:** dark, cinematic, sauce as hero; own icons (the black glove, the sauce pour).
**Don't:** real celebrities' faces or copyrighted worlds — inspired-by, not exact.
